Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE high-fidelity original mobile WeChat mini GAME home-screen static concept for the Chinese Three Kingdoms strategy game “四方夺城”. Full-bleed portrait screen, aspect ratio 375:812 (approximately 1024x2216). No phone device frame, no presentation-board margin, no explanatory callouts. Render the actual screen layout only.

Art direction: “三国城关·出征前庭”, a welcoming but martial ancient Chinese courtyard. Premium hand-painted 2.5D strategy-game illustration, slightly stylized readable miniature architecture and soldiers, crisp compact UI. Muted jade / teal roof tiles, aged warm grey stone, dark timber, modest bronze fittings, vermilion lacquer primary action. Atmospheric painted depth, soft warm daylight, dusty sage distant hills. Antique Three Kingdoms feeling, restrained and original. NOT photorealistic, NOT glossy fantasy MMORPG, NOT a website landing page or admin dashboard. No giant character portraits.

Screen composition with deliberate mobile spacing:
Top 0-5% is quiet sky reserved for device safe area.
At x 7%, y 7.5%, small confident brush-calligraphy game title “四方夺城”, width about 45% of screen. At upper right x 75%, y 7%, a subtle dark translucent WeChat capsule with three dots and circle, small and unobtrusive.
Below title, x 8%, y 14%, a SMALL bronze-and-jade rank insignia plus compact dark text “中郎将”. No large white rank card, no player level number, no progress chart.
Below capsule at x 90%, y 14%, a small tasteful settings gear on a slim circular bronze backing, visual diameter ~26 logical pixels. It is a minor action.
Main artwork from y 18% to 72%: view from slightly above the front courtyard toward an ancient fortified gate. Place broad green-grey tiled gatehouse in upper middle (roof near y 28%, open gate near y 46%). Architecture should occupy about 50% of screen width; its gate opens toward a road and distant hilly landscape. Stone walls, timber guard structures and modest lanterns make a coherent Chinese military garrison. An S-curve stone-and-earth path leads from lower foreground to the gate. Front-left courtyard: a small formation of 5 compact blue-grey armored spear-and-shield soldiers, rounded readable game silhouettes, short proportions but not infantile. Mid-right: 2 patrol soldiers walking on the path. Modest training props and one tethered cavalry horse near a small side shelter. Small figures and architecture feel like the SAME miniature world as a lightweight troop-dispatch game. Do not turn buildings into additional navigation controls. Keep the foreground spacious for the main action. Two subdued banners and light mist suggest life without clutter.
Primary action centered at y 79%, width 55% of screen, height ~56 logical pixels, labeled EXACTLY “出征” in large readable warm ivory brush-like Chinese letters. Original compact vermilion lacquer military-order plaque, subtly chamfered ends, thin aged-bronze edge, modest depth and restrained highlight, NOT a huge gold fantasy frame, NOT a flat full-width web button. Clear dominant action.
At x 88%, y 79%, a much smaller folded-map pictogram for optional battle briefing, without a second large text button.
Bottom navigation centered around y 91%, safely above bottom system area: precisely THREE evenly spaced freestanding compact illustrated icons with dark readable labels underneath: left “练兵” (small training target and spear), middle “策略” (rolled bamboo strategy scroll with jade tie), right “周榜” (bronze victory tablet/laurel motif). Matching hand-painted icon style, ~32-36 logical pixel artwork, no individual large square containers, no thick framed bar, no modern website tabs. A light warm mist/stone ground under these icons ensures contrast. No fourth navigation item.
Leave bottom 3-4% quiet for gesture safe area.

Typography: Only visible words “四方夺城”, “中郎将”, “出征”, “练兵”, “策略”, “周榜”. Chinese text must be correct, crisp and beautifully spaced. Small status and labels should remain comfortably legible at actual phone size. No English words, no fabricated currencies, no VIP, shop, ads, daily quests, red notification dots, reward numbers, fake users, watermarks, captions or dimension annotations. Avoid excessive tiny ornament. No right-side vertical button stack. Overall visual hierarchy: recognizable playable world first, one obvious start action second, quiet secondary navigation third. This is an original static design proposal, not a replica of any reference game.
